## TRIKI Control

The motion engine in `src/gestures/GestureEngine.ts` (gravity and gyro-bias
learning, twist projected onto gravity, stamp/flip/slide detection, gesture
locking and their thresholds), the LED command and the stream-rate start
commands are adapted from TRIKI Control by Wojciech "Koksny" Górny,
https://github.com/koksny/TRIKI-Control. The visual style of this app is
inspired by TRIKI Control's UI. No code or image assets were copied verbatim;
the cap artwork here is drawn separately as vector graphics.
